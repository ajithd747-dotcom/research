**Repo composition note:** crypto-bot's `main` branch is overwhelmingly research prose and captured source material, not code (135 `.md`, 817 `.jpg`, 111 `.txt`, 30 `.json`, 9 `.mp4`, only 1 stray `.py`); its only code lives on the separate `layer0-raw-capture` branch (2,849 lines across 11 modules, 4,640 lines of tests). nse-botonly is the opposite: overwhelmingly real, working code — 587 `.py` files vs. 248 `.md` (mostly design/research prose) — and across all 291 src files opened, zero raw `NotImplementedError`/empty-body stubs were found anywhere. Its main caveat is architectural rather than completeness: a meaningful fraction of its "governance/cognition" modules (conscience/sentience/axiology/society/will packages) are honestly thin rule-lookups or display-only computations dressed in heavy vocabulary, and several live entry-gate levers are wired in but sit at an identity/inert multiplier pending calibration accrual — both patterns are flagged per-row in State/description rather than smoothed over.
