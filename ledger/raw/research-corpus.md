A smaller number are cross-references cited for context rather than true duplicates. Two near-identical FEATURES.md/ARCHITECTURE.md retention-policy rows and one internal placeholder line were caught and merged/removed during a self-review pass before this file was finalized.

Coverage honesty: every table row in FEATURES.md §1–§13 (including the "twenty most-forgotten" and "Phase 0 minimum" summaries, which added no new items beyond what their sections already listed) and every row of ARCHITECTURE.md §5 "Not doing, with reasons" (mandatory per the task) is represented. DESIGN-NOTE and DECISIONS.md were mined for their concrete proposals/decisions, not their narrative reasoning; a small number of argumentative points that support a decision rather than stating a distinct capability (e.g. the "structural advantages of a solo operator" bullets in IDEAS-STRATEGIC.md §2) were folded into the parent row's description rather than split into their own rows — a defensible judgment call, not an omission. ARCHITECTURE.md's own cautionary anecdotes (Alpha Arena, Derwent Capital, survivorship-bias worked example, etc.) were treated as evidence supporting other rows, not as distinct features in their own right, and were not each given a row. SYNTHESIS.md is a superseded file about the Claude Code operating environment rather than the trading system; a representative subset of its content (its explicit "not doing" table and its two live enforcement mechanisms) was captured for completeness since it was named as a source, not exhaustively transcribed. No claim is made that every possible cross-file conceptual overlap beyond the corpus's own explicit self-references was caught — at this volume some residual near-duplicates likely remain.
