**What this repo actually is:** a domain-agnostic ML "model bank" (149-168 node types implementing one common `fit/predict/transform/process` interface) plus mechanisms to connect, route between, evolve, and evaluate those nodes, with a stock/crypto adapter as the only domain-specific surface. Its most important finding (from `capstone.py` / `MODEL_PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md`) is an honest negative result, repeated and reconfirmed dozens of times across the experiment log: predictive edge in this system is DISTRIBUTIONAL/volatility-based, not directional — direction-of-price forecasting tested at chance across essentially every validated model, while volatility/risk forecasting (HAR-RV, Heston, RMT covariance cleaning) shows real, replicated, statistically significant edge.
