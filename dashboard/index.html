Autonomous crypto trading system

Three boards.

One shows what the system is designed to do, one what it is actually doing on the machine right now, and one how far the full build has moved. They are deliberately separate — confusing a plan with a capability is how a plan gets mistaken for one.

Status wall
What is running, broken, stopped or still only designed. Every tile traces to a probe that ran; features with no probe read NOT BUILT.

Feature catalogue
All catalogued features across 15 sections, filterable by phase, with the commonly-forgotten ones flagged.
Measured
Paper blotter
Open positions and closed round trips, read from the forward engine's own fill journal. Every figure under both accountings — optimistic and pessimistic — because a blotter showing one is choosing the flattering one.

Build progress
The full-build master plan, phase by phase — how much of each is lit on the wall, the ledger's UNRESOLVED count, and whether paper trading is journalling. Regenerated with the wall; a dark bar is the correct bar for an unbuilt phase.
